Before an irreversible transition, compare the complete future retained projection against every independent condition that final cleanup will enforce. A correct dependency graph and retired request logs do not prove that retained entry names, their derived comparison keys, metadata or sanitized snapshot recipes contain no selected content-ID literals. Refuse an uncovered retained owner before durable intent and preserve the distinction between identifying a conflict and authorizing removal of that owner.

Independent code advice identified a gap between namespace preflight and the post-retirement database scan. Actual disposable public workflows with a selected content ID in another entry's name, metadata or casefolded comparison key returned an executable preview, then retired the predecessor and became stuck at database-published. Retiring the operation that wrote the entry left the entry itself intact. The bounded correction checks retained names/metadata and replacement manifests before intent, checks retained journal/remotes literal references, and keeps conservative conflicts explicit rather than widening deletion. The existing semantic proof family independently exercises live/deleted/snapshot cases, unchanged refused images, no marker or successor allocation, and successful explicitly corrected closures. The original failed stores, probes and advice remain preserved. These are logical managed-storage guarantees, not physical-media erasure or hardware power-loss qualification.
